## Campaign statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Draft` | Audience and message in progress. |
| `Listed` | Recipient list finalized and **checked against `opt-out.md`**. |
| `Sent` | The mailing has gone out. |
| `Followed up` | Follow-up sent to non-responders. |
| `Closed` | No further sends in this campaign. Replies broken out to `prospects.md`. |

## Before anything is sent

1. The recipient list is checked against [opt-out.md](./opt-out.md).
2. Every address is a company address or a named person in their professional
   role - no private addresses without prior consent.
3. The message contains a working way to decline further contact.
4. The sender is clearly identified: «company name», «registration number»,
   «contact address».
